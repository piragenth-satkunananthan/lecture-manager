## Providers

Different companies host the same model. OpenRouter routes your request to one
of them based on the routing mode you pick — Balanced (price + speed), Nitro
(fastest), or Floor (cheapest).

## Performance

Throughput is how fast the model writes (tokens per second — higher is
better). Latency is total round-trip time (lower is better). TTFT is time-to-
first-token — how long before you see anything appear (lower is better).

## Uptime

Uptime is the percentage of the past 3 days that at least one provider was
responding to requests. Availability is the percentage of time that inference
was successfully served. OpenRouter continuously monitors and uses the next-
best provider when one returns an error.

## Benchmarks

Scores on standardized evaluations and this model’s own outputs on shared
benchmark prompts. Higher percentages are better — and each rank shows where
this model lands among the models evaluated on that source.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is Gemma 4 31B (free)?

Gemma 4 31B Instruct is Google DeepMind's 30.7B dense multimodal model
supporting text and image input with text output. Features a 256K token
context window, configurable thinking/reasoning mode, native function calling,
and multilingual support across 140+ languages. Strong on coding, reasoning,
and document understanding tasks. Apache 2.0 license.

### Is Gemma 4 31B (free) free?

Yes. The pricing shown on this page for Gemma 4 31B (free) is zero, so you are not charged for prompt or completion tokens. Free endpoints are rate limited — see the [rate limit docs](/docs/api-reference/limits).

### What is the context length of Gemma 4 31B (free)?

Gemma 4 31B (free) has a 262,144 token context window. It supports up to
32,768 completion tokens.

### Does Gemma 4 31B (free) support tool calling and structured outputs?

Yes. Gemma 4 31B (free) accepts `tools` and `tool_choice` for function
calling. It supports `response_format` for JSON output, without JSON-schema
enforcement.

### What inputs and outputs does Gemma 4 31B (free) support?

Gemma 4 31B (free) accepts images, text, and video as input and returns text.

### What other text models does Google have?

[Gemini Omni 1.2 Flash](/google/gemini-omni-1.2-flash), [Gemini Omni 1.1 Flash](/google/gemini-omni-1.1-flash), [Gemini 4 Argon](/google/gemini-4-argon) and 28 more are other text models from Google.

### When was Gemma 4 31B (free) released?

Gemma 4 31B (free) was released on April 2, 2026.
